Privacy Policy

Support Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026

In short

SinceBoard does not collect any data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app does not do

  • It collects no personal data and no usage data.
  • It has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • It makes no network requests.
  • It contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party software kits.
  • It does not track you, in the app or across other apps and websites.

The data you enter

The items you create, their schedules, notes and the dates you marked them as done are stored only on your device. The developer cannot see or access them. All of this data is removed when you delete the app.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications that your iPhone schedules and shows itself. They are not sent through any server.

Device backups

If you back up your iPhone, for example with iCloud Backup or to a computer, the app's data may be included in that backup. Those backups are handled by Apple under your own settings and Apple's privacy policy, not by SinceBoard.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, scripts or trackers of its own. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address when you visit; see GitHub's privacy statement for how it handles that.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
