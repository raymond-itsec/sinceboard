What the app does

SinceBoard is a small, free iPhone app for the things you only do now and then: changing the water filter, descaling the coffee machine, washing the car. It shows how long ago you last did each of them and, if you give an item a schedule, whether it is due again.

Mark something as done with one swipe, look back at its history, and let the app remind you on the day it is due. There is no account and nothing to set up.

Questions and answers

  • Where is my data?

    Only on this iPhone. The app has no server and no account.

  • Does it sync between devices?

    No. Each device keeps its own list.

  • How do reminders work?

    If you switch on the reminder for an item, the app schedules a local notification for 09:00 on the day the item is due. The reminder is created on your iPhone; nothing is sent over the internet. The first time you switch a reminder on, iOS asks whether SinceBoard may show notifications.

  • I am not getting reminders. What can I check?

    Open the Settings app, go to Notifications, choose SinceBoard and make sure notifications are allowed. Also check that the item has a schedule and that its reminder is switched on.

  • Can I record something I did on an earlier day?

    Yes. Open the item and choose "Done on another date...". You can pick any day from 1 January 2025 up to today.

  • How do I delete everything?

    Delete the app. All of its data is removed with it.
